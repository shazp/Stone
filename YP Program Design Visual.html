Placement
Employment or postsecondary + retention follow-up
Foundational tech

Fortifi

IT support, web technology, digital media & project-management basics with strong professional development. Fast on-ramp; heavy wraparound; serves many justice-impacted youth.

Recommended credentials: Google IT Support · CompTIA A+ (stretch) · freeCodeCamp Web Design
Advanced tech

Codetalk

Immersive, project-based training in UX design, agile project management, and technology-enabled problem-solving. Builds a portfolio for a tech career, not just a first job.

Recommended credentials: Google UX Design · Google/PMI Project Management (CAPM) · Agile/Scrum
