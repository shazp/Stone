About This Dashboard

What SLA Works is for, and how to use it.

Purpose

A single, always-current reference for the scope, progress, and milestones of Youth Pathways South LA. Open it any time you want to know where the project stands — no need to wait for a status meeting.

How it stays current

Updated every Monday morning, and immediately after major developments. The “Updated” date at the top right tells you exactly how fresh what you're reading is.

Reading it in 30 seconds

The banner tells you overall health. Milestones show trajectory to the Nov 4 launch. Current Priorities show this week's focus. Everything below is depth when you want it.

Have input?

This dashboard is two-way. If you want something added, tracked, or corrected — or want deeper detail on any item — tell Sharon and it will appear in the next update.

What it is not

This is a management view, not the system of record. Participant-level data and outcome reporting live in Salesforce (build in progress, with the Data team) and will feed the Outcomes section from launch onward.

Maintained by

Sharon Plunkett, Project Lead — Youth Pathways South LA. Questions, corrections, and requests welcome any time.
